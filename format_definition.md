# Content Format: Business Storytelling Reels

## Description
Short vertical videos (60-100 seconds) where on-camera presenters reveal the
"hidden engineering" behind companies, personal brands, and cultural phenomena.
Each video follows a hook → context → plot twist → practical application → CTA
structure. The channel targets social media and marketing professionals in Brazil.

## Key Elements
- 1-3 on-camera presenters telling a story
- Plot twist / revelation structure ("A Grande Sacada")
- Topics: company histories, marketing strategies, brand positioning, pop culture crossovers
- Tone: provocative, engaging, colloquial Brazilian Portuguese
- Always ends with a CTA mentioning Genna

## Presenters
- presenter_a (main male presenter)
- presenter_b (main female presenter)
- presenter_c (second female presenter)
- Occasionally guests or combinations

## What varies between good and bad posts?
- Strength and surprise factor of the "big idea" / plot twist
- Whether the subject is a company, a person, a strategy, or a cultural trend
- Presenter energy, delivery style, and on-camera chemistry
- Whether the story is relatable to the target audience (social media professionals)
- Balance between entertainment and education
- Whether the topic is business-focused or pop-culture-focused
- How well-known the subject is to the audience
- Whether specific numbers, revenue figures, or growth stats are cited
- Complexity of the narrative (single arc vs. layered)
- How strong the hook is at stopping the scroll

## Typical Script Structure
- [Hook — 0 to 6s] — bold claim or surprising fact
- [Context — 6 to 18s] — sets up the story
- [The Big Reveal — 18 to 60s] — plot twist, the longest section
- [Practical Application — 60 to 82s] — what the audience can learn from this
- [CTA — 82 to 95s] — always mentions Genna
